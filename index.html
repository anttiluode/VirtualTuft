The delay becomes 50 virtual nodes

Columns show the 50 successive time slots of one nonlinear unit; rows are successive input cycles (newest at bottom). They are not 50 physical neurons, or a spatial cortical field.

What this does and does not show. The input worlds are simulations, not recordings from Shaw's faucet. The blue readout is trained online from earlier observed intervals; its future predictions are evaluated causally. The optional feedback adaptation is a local sensory-echo correlation rule, not evidence of biological myelin or apical-tuft plasticity.
